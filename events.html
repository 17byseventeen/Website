School Clubs

A network of youth-led community action across Canada

Start a 17 by Seventeen club

17 by Seventeen is a youth-led organization that helps young people turn ideas into action. Inspired by the United Nations’ 17 Sustainable Development Goals, school clubs give students a way to lead community initiatives, build partnerships, and take on real leadership.

A club can be brand new, or an existing school group joining the network. Either way, registering formally connects your school with the national organization so we can stay in touch, offer support, and recognize what your club accomplishes.

What a club is

Student-led

Members develop and organize initiatives themselves. A teacher advisor guides and supervises, while students keep ownership of projects and decisions.

Aligned with the SDGs

Clubs choose the Sustainable Development Goals that matter in their community and plan projects around those priorities.

Part of a network

Registered clubs stay in contact with the national organization, share updates, and report the impact of their work.

How to register

Complete the school club registration package and submit it to the national 17 by Seventeen organization. Every section is required.

  1. School and club details

    School name, address, city or town, province or territory, postal code, website, and phone number. Also include the proposed club name (for example, 17 by Seventeen – [School Name]), the date it is established, how many founding members you expect, and whether this is a new club or an existing group.

  2. Teacher advisor

    Every club names a teacher or school staff member as advisor: full name, position or department, school email, and an optional phone number. The advisor mentors student leaders, helps with meetings and school approvals, makes sure activities follow school and safeguarding policies, and is the link between the club, the school, and the national organization.

  3. Student executive

    List each founding executive: full name, grade, school email, and a preferred contact email. Clubs may add extra positions if they need them.

  4. Objectives

    Share why your school wants a club, which SDGs you want to work on, the kinds of initiatives you expect to run, any community partners you hope to work with, and whether you plan to meet weekly, biweekly, or monthly.

  5. Contacts and agreements

    Name a primary and secondary club contact. Add Instagram or other social accounts if you have them. Public accounts that use the 17 by Seventeen name follow the organization’s branding guidelines. The president, teacher advisor, and school administration (if your school requires it) sign the registration declaration.

Student leadership team

The executive coordinates meetings, communication, and projects, and represents the club with the national organization.

President

  • Oversee club operations and organization
  • Coordinate meetings and set agendas
  • Delegate work and support project development
  • Represent the club with the national organization

Vice-President

  • Assist the president with club operations
  • Help organize meetings and initiatives
  • Help maintain continuity within the club

Communications Coordinator

  • Coordinate communication inside the club
  • Share updates and promote upcoming initiatives
  • Support communication with school and community partners

Project Coordinator

  • Help plan and organize initiatives
  • Coordinate timelines and responsibilities
  • Track progress and outcomes

What clubs agree to

Student leadership

Members develop and organize initiatives, with appropriate adult guidance.

Organizational alignment

The club supports the mission of 17 by Seventeen and works on the Sustainable Development Goals.

Communication

The club stays in touch with the national organization and shares updates on its activities.

Activity reporting

The club submits activity and impact reports through the 17 by Seventeen reporting form.

Branding

Public activities and communications represent 17 by Seventeen according to the branding guidelines.

Safety and privacy

Activities follow school policies and safeguarding rules. Clubs get permission before sharing identifiable photos, personal information, or student stories.

Report an action or initiative

Once your club is running, complete this form each time the club carries out an action or initiative. It is how the national organization hears what your school has done and recognizes your club’s contribution.
